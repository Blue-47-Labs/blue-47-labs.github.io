CRAB AND SHRIMP POT TRACKER

Dedicated pot tracking and analytics.Without the waypoint busywork.

Keep your map focused on the pots you currently have in the water, without manually cleaning up GPS waypoints. Track locations, soak times, and catches in one place. Use catch analytics and a heatmap to see what’s working and where you’re catching the most.

For iPhone & iPad · Crab, shrimp & lobster

01 · FIND YOUR POTS

Drop with confidence. Return with ease.

Save pot locations and depths, then use the map and compass to find your way back. Return to your best spots on future outings.

02 · TRACK YOUR CATCH

See where you’re catching the most.

Record catches, soak times, and notes for each pot. Use the catch heatmap to spot your most productive areas.

03 · LEARN FROM EVERY PULL

Find what works. Keep getting better.

Explore catch and pot performance statistics to compare results and learn from past outings.
