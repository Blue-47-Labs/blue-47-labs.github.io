Traffic Time

Know when you’ll arrive.Before you head out.

See travel times to your favorite destinations at a glance. Check the fastest route, keep an ETA on your Home Screen, and let someone know when to expect you—all with less tapping.

App support

For iPhone, iPad & Mac · Widgets & iMessage

01 · CHECK YOUR JOURNEY

Your regular places. One quick look.

See saved destinations together with the fastest route, or explore travel times from your current location on the map.

02 · KEEP IT HANDY

An ETA within easy reach.

Add a Home Screen widget to keep travel times visible. Allow extra time for walking to the car or finding a parking spot.

03 · SHARE YOUR ARRIVAL

Keep them in the loop.

Send your ETA directly in iMessage. Your saved destinations also stay in sync across iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
