ShallWe Decider

Less back and forth.More plans together.

Turn a group conversation into a plan. Share options in iMessage, give everyone a chance to vote, and find a choice the group can get behind.

App support

For iPhone & iPad · Made for iMessage

01 · SHARE THE OPTIONS

Start with a few good ideas.

Picking a place to eat? Put the options into your conversation so everyone has something to choose from.

02 · INCLUDE EVERYONE

Give the group a voice.

Let people vote on the options, keeping the decision in the same conversation as the plan.

03 · MAKE A PLAN

Find your next yes.

Choose a time to get together or find out who’s interested in an activity. Keep everyday decisions moving.
